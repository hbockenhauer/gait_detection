# Description of the Clinical Dataset 
The dataset is recorded on the patients recovering from stroke at the Rijndam Revalidatiecentrum. 
## Subject 1 
Patient was using a walker. The right side was affected. Patient had a stable mobility level.  
Patient performed the following activities: 
- Sitting 
- Walking to kitchen area
- Washing their hands 
- Walking to the exercise room
- Folding laundry while sitting 
- Walking back to the common room
- Putting together domino pieces (while sitting)
- Walking around the building 
- Reading/flipping through a magazine (while sitting)
- Walking to the plants area
- Watering the plants 
- Walking back to the chair 

Two short stops in first and third walking periods were also annotated. During watering the plants, the patient first walked to the watering can, stopped to pick it up, walked to the plants with stopping several times (these shorter bouts were labelled as non-walking), and walked back.

## Subject 2 
Patient was using a walker. The left side was affected, patient had a low mobility level. The walking speed was much slower than for patient 1.  
Patient performed the following activities: 
- Sitting down
- Walking to the kitchen area
- Washing their hands
- Walking back to the table 
- Putting together domino pieces (while sitting)
- Walking to the watering can 
- Watering the plants 
- Walking back to the chair 

The watering period was annotated as non-walking as the walking bouts were very short and difficult to annotate. The transitions between walking/non-walking were difficult to annotate with the exact moment. 

## Subject 3 
Patient was walking unassisted. The left side was affected. High mobility level. 
Patient performed the following activities: 
- Sitting down
- Walking to the kitchen area
- Washing their hands
- Walking back to the table 
- Putting together domino pieces (while sitting)
- Walking around the building 
- Reading/flipping through a magazine (while sitting)
- Walking to the exercise room 
- Using the machine for pedalling with their arms 
- Walking to the plants area
- Watering the plants
- Walking back to the chair 

The period of stopping to reach for the watering can is labelled as non-walking. The walking bouts in between the different plants are labelled as non-walking due to them being short.

## Subject 4 
Patient was using a cane with the right arm. The left side was affected. Patient had a good mobility level for both sides. 
Patient performed the following activities: 
- Standing
- Walking to the exercise room 
- Folding laundry 
- Walking back 
- Putting together puzzle pieces (while sitting)
- Walking around the building 
- Sitting down 


## Subject 5
Patient was using a cane with the right arm. The left side was affected. Relied more heavily on the unaffected side compared to patient 4. 
Patient performed the following activities: 
- Sitting down
- Walking to the exercise room 
- Folding laundry 
- Walking to the kitchen area
- Washing and drying their hands
- Walking back to the table
- Putting together puzzle pieces (while sitting)
- Walking around the building 
- Watering the plants
- Walking to the chair
- Sitting down 

The watering period was annotated as non-walking despite having short walking bouts.
