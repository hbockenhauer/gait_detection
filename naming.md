# The naming used for multimobility_wrist_qsense.py
The files names startign with W are assumed to only have walking activity. 
First letters indicate the activity: 
* W = Walking 
* WS = Walking on stairs 
* S = Standing 
* L = Laptop/sitting at the desk

The letter afterwards indicates the participant name. s1_1RW are from the right wrist and s3_3ST are from the left wrist.
